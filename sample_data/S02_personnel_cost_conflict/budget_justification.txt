
GRANT APPLICATION BUDGET JUSTIFICATION
Application ID: GC-S02
Document Type: Budget Justification

Project Title: Coastal Water Quality Monitoring

PROJECT PERIOD
The proposed project will be conducted over a 24-month period.

PERSONNEL JUSTIFICATION
The project will employ one Graduate Research Assistant
at 50% effort throughout the project period.

The total requested personnel cost for the Graduate
Research Assistant is $20,000.

TRAVEL JUSTIFICATION
A total of $8,000 is requested for project-related travel.

These funds will support travel to coastal monitoring
sites for field data collection and participation in
scientific conferences to present research findings.
