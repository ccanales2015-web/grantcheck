
GRANT APPLICATION PROJECT NARRATIVE
Application ID: GC-S07
Document Type: Project Narrative

Project Title: Coastal Water Quality Monitoring

PROJECT OVERVIEW
This project will investigate coastal water quality
through field observations and environmental data analysis.

The research will be conducted over two years, with
monitoring activities scheduled throughout the project
period.

PROJECT ACTIVITIES
During the first year, the research team will establish
monitoring locations, collect baseline water quality
measurements, and develop data analysis procedures.

During the second year, the team will continue field
monitoring, analyze the collected measurements, and
prepare research publications.

PERSONNEL
A Graduate Research Assistant will support field data
collection, data management, and statistical analysis.

EXPECTED OUTCOMES
The project will produce a coastal water quality dataset,
an analysis of observed environmental trends, and
research findings suitable for scientific publication.
